eml(x,y) = exp(x) − ln(y)

1 puts a 1 on the stack. eml takes the two newest numbers, x then y (y is the newest), and puts exp(x) − ln(y) back.

Try: 1 1 eml gives e. 1 1 eml 1 eml gives ee. Results can be complex, since ln of a negative number is allowed. Keyboard: 1, e or Enter for eml, Backspace to undo, Esc to clear.
